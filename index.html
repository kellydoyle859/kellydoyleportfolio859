Social Media Analytics & Content Strategy

LinkedIn Analytics Report — Writing Style vs. Content Type

The marketing team wanted to improve engagement and CTR on the firm's LinkedIn posts — so I built an analytics report to find out what was actually driving performance.

TaskIdentify what was driving content performance to inform a smarter, more effective publishing strategy.
ActionAnalyzed 47 posts across content type and writing style; found that writing approach outperformed content category as a performance driver. Packaged findings into an opportunity roadmap, presented to the team, and led writing workshops to teach a more differentiated approach.
ResultDifferentiated posts drove 9.8% engagement and 7.15% CTR vs. 5.86% and 3.28% for templated language — roughly 67% higher engagement and 2x+ CTR. Findings led to firmwide writing workshops and a new internal process for how social posts are written going forward.
